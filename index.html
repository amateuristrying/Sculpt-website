01 — Source

Start with one photo.

Drop a PNG, JPG or WEBP of a single object. The original stays in your local library.

02 — Foreground

Isolate the object.

Preview the mask before anything runs. Paint to add or erase wherever it guessed wrong.

03 — Reconstruct

Lift it into 3D.

TripoSR infers the full shape on your Mac's GPU through Metal — including the sides the camera never saw.

04 — Refine

Clean up the geometry.

Re-extract at higher detail, drop stray fragments and smooth the surface — without running the model again.

05 — Texture & export

Bake it. Ship it.

Bake a 1K or 2K color texture, then export GLB, glTF, OBJ, or STL at a real-world size.

Results

Straight out of Sculpt.

Drag to turn them. Switch views the way you would in the app.

Honest beta

What to expect.

Works best with

  • One clear object per photo
  • A plain, uncluttered background
  • Even light, the whole object in frame
  • Solid forms — vases, toys, props, figures

Still hard

  • Thin parts like straps, wires and leaves
  • Hidden backs the photo can't show
  • Busy scenes with several objects
  • Game-ready topology out of the box

We'd rather you know now. Every beta report makes the next engine better — tell us what broke.
